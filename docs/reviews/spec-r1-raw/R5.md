Summary: The spec records consent and logs views of personal data, but it has no rules for most of the other trust questions. These are:
- what third parties (Hina's vendor, the intent reader, the geocoder, the WhatsApp provider) may hold, and who signs for it;
- how the SUL's personal phone is protected, and what happens to it when an SUL leaves;
- password and session rules;
- how long unattached signals and raw payloads are kept;
- what role DBO logs in as;
- how an identity merged in error is detected;
- whether an unverified number can key a grower.

The three blockers:
- A resigned or moved SUL keeps the territory's personal data offline. This breaks INV-7.
- There is no device protection on the personal phone.
- A WhatsApp STOP sits against a no-action rule, with no Legal sign-off recorded against PECA 2016 s.25(2) or Meta's opt-out rule.
